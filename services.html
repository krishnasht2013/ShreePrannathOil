How it works

  1. Call or message us

    Ask the pressing fee, tell us what seed you have, and check that the mill is open that day.

  2. Bring your seed to the mill

    Come to Belsi, Ratnanagar-3 during opening hours. You can open our location in Google Maps from the contact page.

  3. We press it

    We press your seed at the mill for the fee we agreed with you.

  4. Collect your oil

    Take your oil home.
