SAVE EMAILS TO A GOOGLE SHEET (free, about 10 minutes)

Your website is a static site, so it has no database. The easiest free way to
keep emails is a Google Sheet. You can download it as Excel at any time
(File > Download > Microsoft Excel).

1. Go to sheets.google.com and create a new blank spreadsheet.
   Name it "Shree Prannath email list".

2. In the sheet, click Extensions > Apps Script.

3. Delete everything in the editor. Open the file google-sheet-script.gs
   from this folder, copy all of it, and paste it into the editor.
   Click the Save icon.

4. Click Deploy > New deployment.
   - Click the gear icon next to "Select type" and choose "Web app".
   - Description: Email list
   - Execute as: Me
   - Who has access: Anyone
   Click Deploy. Google asks you to authorize. Click "Authorize access", choose
   your Google account, then Advanced > "Go to ... (unsafe)" > Allow.
   (The warning appears because you wrote the script yourself.)

5. Copy the "Web app URL". It starts with https://script.google.com/macros/s/

6. Open contact.html in VS Code. Find this line near the bottom:
       var SHEET_URL = "";
   Paste the URL between the quotes, then save the file.

7. Upload to GitHub as usual. Test it: submit the "Join the email list" form on
   your contact page. A new row should appear in the "Leads" tab of your sheet.

NOTES
- Only people who tick the email box (order form) or use the "Join the email
  list" form are saved. Their consent is recorded in the last column.
- If you change the script later, use Deploy > Manage deployments > Edit >
  New version, otherwise the website keeps using the old version.
- Anyone who has the Web app URL can send rows to your sheet. The script only
  accepts rows that tick consent and have a valid email, but if you ever get
  spam, create a new deployment and replace the URL in contact.html.
- To send the actual emails, use the sheet with a tool such as Mailchimp,
  Brevo or Gmail mail merge. Always include a way to unsubscribe.
